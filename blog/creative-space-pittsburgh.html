The Creative Space Where Pittsburgh Event Pros Work

Updated July 2026 · For photographers, planners & makers

Pittsburgh has coworking for laptops. What it never had is a workspace for the people who make events: photographers who need shoot space, planners who need a client-meeting spot that isn't a coffee shop, content creators who need backdrops that change monthly. Our converted firehouse in Blawnox is exactly that.

What a creative space for event pros looks like

  • Shoot-ready sets — professionally built balloon installs, shimmer walls and floral backdrops rotate through the space constantly, because our decor studio builds them here. Book a weekday and shoot against work that clients pay hundreds for.
  • Natural light + neutral bones — the firehouse's clean tones mean your brand colors (or your client's) always fit.
  • Real logistics — free on-site parking, loading access, and space to spread out a build or a full flat-lay production.
  • The network effect — the building already hums with event people: installers, florists, planners. Collaborations start in the hallway.

Who it's for

  • Photographers — mini-session days, branding shoots, maternity and milestone sessions with sets you didn't have to build.
  • Content creators — a month of content in an afternoon, with backdrops that don't look like your living room.
  • Event planners & stylists — client tastings, mood-board meetings and mockups in a space that sells the vision for you.
  • Small product brands — launch shoots and pop-up staging with the decor team one door away.

How to book it

Weekday rates are the lever: the full space rents for $1,500 on weekdays — split a creative day with two or three other pros and it beats studio rates around the city, with far better sets. Hosting a workshop or a client event instead? The same room hosts up to 100 guests.

Grow-your-business angle: event pros who shoot here end up referring clients to each other. The space pays for itself the first time a planner books your photography because they met you at a shoot day.
