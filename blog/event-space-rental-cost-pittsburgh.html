How Much Does It Cost to Rent an Event Space in Pittsburgh?

Updated July 2026 · Real prices, no vagueness

Short answer: in 2026, most private event spaces in Pittsburgh rent for $500 to $5,000+ per event, depending on the day of the week, guest count and what's actually included. Here's how the market breaks down — including our own published rates, since we think venues that hide pricing waste everyone's time.

Pittsburgh event space pricing by venue type

  • Community rooms & fire halls: $200–$800. Cheapest option; expect folding tables, DIY everything, and dated interiors in photos.
  • Dedicated private event spaces (like ours): $1,000–$3,500 per day. You get the whole space, modern finishes and real flexibility.
  • Restaurant private rooms & rooftops: often "free" rental but with food & beverage minimums of $2,000–$10,000 — the room isn't the cost, the catering contract is.
  • Museums, historic halls & premium wedding venues: $4,000–$15,000+ once rental, required vendors and staffing stack up.

Our actual rates (published, flat, no games)

At Party on Butler's converted firehouse in Blawnox, venue pricing is one flat rate for the full day, up to 100 guests:

  • Weekdays (Mon–Fri): $1,500
  • Sundays: $2,000
  • Saturdays: $3,000

That includes full access to the venue, free on-site parking and — right now — a complimentary balloon backdrop. A 50% deposit locks the date (non-refundable, but fully transferable if you reschedule).

The hidden fees to ask about (anywhere you book)

  • Cleaning fees — $150–$500, often revealed after the tour.
  • Required vendor lists — some venues force their caterer or bar, at their prices.
  • Hourly overages — "8-hour rental" venues charging $200–$400/hr the minute your DJ runs long.
  • Parking — Downtown, your guests may pay $15–$30 each to attend. Free lots are worth real money.
  • Decor restrictions — no-tape, no-candle, no-confetti rules that quietly kill your Pinterest board.
Rule of thumb: price the whole event, not the room. A "cheap" $600 hall plus rentals, decor delivery fees and paid parking often costs more than a $1,500 venue with those things built in.

How to save money on a Pittsburgh event space

  1. Go off-Saturday. Our weekday rate is literally half of Saturday. Sunday brunches are the underrated move for showers.
  2. Bundle venue + decor. One vendor, one delivery fee, one timeline. (It's the whole reason our decor studio and venue share a building.)
  3. Skip venues with F&B minimums for casual parties — you'll overbuy catering to hit the number.
