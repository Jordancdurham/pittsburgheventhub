FAQ

Everything People Ask Before Booking

How many guests can the event space hold?
Up to 100 guests, depending on your layout — seated dinners, cocktail-style receptions, showers and workshops all fit differently. Tell us your headcount and we'll suggest a layout.
How much does it cost to rent?
$3,000 Saturdays · $2,000 Sundays · $1,500 weekdays (Mon–Fri). That's full access to the venue with no hidden fees — and right now booking includes a complimentary balloon backdrop. Full breakdown in our Pittsburgh event space cost guide.
Where are you located? Is there parking?
1341 Old Freeport Road, Blawnox, PA — a converted firehouse about 20 minutes from Downtown Pittsburgh. Free on-site parking is included, which almost no in-city venue can offer.
How do I book, and what's the deposit?
A 50% deposit secures your date. It's non-refundable but fully transferable if you need to reschedule. Call (412) 871-6102 or email contact@partyonbutler.com.
Does the rental include furniture or decor?
The rental covers the venue itself. Furniture isn't included — we'll recommend trusted rental partners or handle it through our planning add-on. Decor is our superpower: balloon installations, backdrops, marquee letters and florals are all made in-house.
Can you plan the whole event for me?
Yes. Full-service event planning is available as an add-on — vendor coordination, timeline, layout design and day-of logistics, run by the team that's produced 1,000+ Pittsburgh events.
What kinds of events can I host?
Weddings, baby and bridal showers, milestone birthdays, corporate gatherings, photo shoots, brand activations and more. If it's a celebration, it fits.
